About

Sitting in Clearness was made by Stephen Chen as part of the work of White Knight Law Inc., a nonprofit that builds open-source tools to serve people. He makes AI that supports human judgment rather than replacing it.

Questions, a correction, or a quotation we got wrong? Write to ai@whiteknightlaw.org.

Where your words go. What you write is kept on this device. To form each question, your words are sent through OpenRouter to an open-source AI model. Our code stores and logs nothing, but the companies that run free AI models may keep or learn from what is sent to them. So please leave out names and anything you would not want seen. “Lay this down” erases everything from this device.

This is not a clearness committee and cannot replace one. For a weighty matter, ask your meeting.
